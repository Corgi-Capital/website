Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

1. Overview

Corgi Capital ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard information collected through our website corgi-capital.com and the Corgi Capital Winston Alerts SMS program.

2. Information We Collect

When you opt in to the SMS alerts program, we collect:

  • Mobile phone number (required for SMS delivery)
  • Email address (for account recovery and support)
  • IP address (from the opt-in form submission, for fraud prevention)
  • Timestamp of opt-in (for compliance records)

We do not collect any other personally identifiable information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • Delivering operational SMS alerts you have requested
  • Maintaining consent records as required by FCC and CTIA regulations
  • Responding to your support requests and opt-out instructions
  • Preventing abuse, fraud, and unauthorized use of our system

We do NOT share, sell, rent, or transfer your mobile phone number or any opt-in data to third parties, affiliates, or advertising partners for any purpose, including marketing or promotional activities.

4. Information Sharing

Your mobile information and opt-in data will never be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Amazon Web Services (AWS) Pinpoint / End User Messaging to deliver SMS. AWS processes messages as a service provider subject to its own privacy policy.
  • Legal compliance: If required by law, court order, or valid legal process.

5. Data Retention

We retain opt-in records (phone number, email, timestamp, IP) for as long as you remain subscribed, plus one additional year after opt-out, to comply with telecommunications regulations. After this period, we delete your data from our systems.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message or emailing bill@corgi-capital.com
  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to retention requirements)
  • Update your information by contacting us

7. Data Security

We store opt-in data in encrypted databases hosted on AWS with access restricted to the sole operator. We use TLS encryption for data in transit. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we follow industry standards to protect your information.

8. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the SMS program after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
