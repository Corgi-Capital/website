1. Program Overview

Corgi Capital ("we," "us," or "our") operates an automated quantitative trading system called Winston. The Corgi Capital Winston Alerts program is a private, invitation-only SMS service. Recipients include the system operator and a small group of invited friends who have explicitly requested access and consented to receive messages.

2. Consent to Receive Messages

To join the program, you must submit the opt-in form at corgi-capital.com/sms-opt-in by providing your mobile phone number and checking the consent box. Submissions are reviewed manually by the operator, and only invited individuals are added to the active subscriber list. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

3. Message Content

Messages fall into two categories:

  • Operational notifications (to the operator only): kill switch activations, post-trade audit reviews requiring KEEP or EXIT decisions, and infrastructure health warnings.
  • Notable trading activity updates (to invited friends): informational updates about significant positions, market events, or trade outcomes shared at the operator's discretion.

All messages are informational and system- or operator-generated. We do not send marketing, promotional, advertising, or third-party content. Trading activity updates are not investment advice and do not constitute a recommendation or solicitation to buy, sell, or hold any security, contract, or financial instrument.
